Cost My Trip

Estimate your fuel and electricity costs for any journey

About this tool

Cost My Trip is a free UK journey cost calculator. Estimate petrol, diesel and electric charging costs for any trip in seconds. Figures are for guidance only — actual prices and consumption may vary.

How to use

1. Select your vehicle type: Petrol/Diesel or Electric
2. Enter your journey distance in miles
3. Enter your vehicle's consumption
4. Enter the price — choose pence or pounds
5. Click "Calculate Cost"
