Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who we are

Cost My Trip is a free online calculator tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Data controller. The data controller for this website is the owner and operator of Cost My Trip. You can contact us at any time at whichischeaperapp@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

We do not require you to create an account or submit personal information to use our calculator. The numbers you enter into the calculator are processed only in your browser and are not stored on our servers.

We do not use web analytics, advertising pixels, fingerprinting, or similar tracking, and we do not ask for names, email addresses, account details, or payment information. The figures you enter (distance, consumption, price, and any optional parking or toll cost) are used only to perform the calculation in your browser; they are not sent to us or to any third party.

3. Cookies and local storage

This site uses only a functional appearance preference: a small first-party cookie and browser local storage (cmt-theme) set when you choose light or dark mode. It sets no analytics or advertising cookies and no third-party tracking cookies. For details, see our Cookies page.

4. Third-party services and hosting

This website does not use Google Analytics, Google AdSense, or any other advertising or analytics service, and no visitor data is shared with advertisers or data brokers. No third-party widgets, maps, comment forms, or embeds are loaded.

Hosting. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company). GitHub may process standard technical data — such as your IP address and browser type — in server logs solely to provide, maintain, and secure the hosting service. GitHub acts as a processor for these hosting logs under its own privacy statement; we do not have access to visitor-level server logs.

Legal basis. The only device-side data is the appearance preference you choose yourself (light/dark mode), which is stored to provide the display option you requested. We run no consent-based advertising or analytics processing.

International transfers. The site itself does not transfer personal data outside the United Kingdom or the EEA. Hosting logs are handled by GitHub under GitHub's own privacy terms and applicable law.

Data retention. We hold no user accounts, visitor databases, or server-side personal data. Your calculator entries never leave your device. The theme preference remains in your browser until you clear your cookies or site data; hosting-provider logs follow GitHub's retention policy.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port personal data concerning you, and to object to processing. Because we do not hold personal data about visitors, there is nothing for us to retrieve or export; deleting your browser data removes the local appearance preference.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) — see ico.org.uk — if you believe our handling of your data has breached data protection law.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at: whichischeaperapp@gmail.com
