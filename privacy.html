Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Cost My Trip is a free online calculator tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

2. Data we collect

We do not require you to create an account or submit personal information to use our calculator. The numbers you enter into the calculator are processed only in your browser and are not stored on our servers.

We may collect anonymous analytics data (such as pages visited, approximate country, and device type) to understand how visitors use our site and improve our service.

3. Cookies

We use essential cookies to make the website work and analytics cookies to understand usage. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the banner shown on our site. For more details, see our Cookies page.

4. Third-party services

We use Google Analytics 4 to analyse traffic and Google AdSense to display advertisements. Both are loaded via Google Consent Mode v2: non-essential cookie storage is denied by default and is only activated after you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics or advertising cookies are set (Google may still receive anonymous, cookieless, aggregated pings). These services are governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Google AdSense: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site only after consent. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Since we do not store personal data, most requests can be fulfilled by simply clearing your browser cookies.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at: whichischeaperapp@gmail.com
