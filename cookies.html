Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. The UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) require consent before placing non-essential cookies, such as advertising or analytics cookies. Cookies that are strictly necessary for a service you have asked for may be set without a consent banner.

What this site uses

This calculator sets no advertising cookies, no analytics or measurement cookies, and no third-party tracking cookies. There are no Google Analytics, Google AdSense, or similar tags on this site. The only device-side preference is your chosen light/dark appearance:

  • Functional preference cookie — cmt-theme (first party, up to 365 days). Set only when you use the theme toggle; it remembers whether you prefer light or dark mode. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking.
  • Browser local storage (localStorage) — cmt-theme stores the same appearance preference in your browser. Local storage stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.

The numbers you enter in the calculator (distance, consumption, price, and any optional parking or toll cost) are processed entirely in your browser. They are not saved in cookies, local storage, or any server database.

No consent banner

Because the site sets no non-essential cookies, no Accept/Decline consent banner is required under PECR, and none is shown. The calculator works fully even if you block or delete cookies.

Managing cookies and local storage

You can delete or block the functional theme cookie and local storage at any time through your browser settings; every major browser lets you remove individual cookies or clear site data. Doing so only resets the appearance preference — the calculator and all results work normally. If cookies are blocked, the site simply uses your system appearance each visit.

Third-party services and hosting

This site does not use analytics or advertising services and does not share visitor data with advertisers or data brokers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and request time) to operate and secure the hosting service, under GitHub's own privacy statement. No third-party widgets, maps, comment forms, or embeds are loaded.
